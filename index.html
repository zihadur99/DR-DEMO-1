Meet Dr. Mariam

A doctor by training. A mama by heart.

I became a family doctor because I love caring for people across every chapter of life: the new parent up at 2 a.m., the teenager with questions they're nervous to ask, the grandparent juggling five prescriptions. Family medicine lets me know the whole story, not just the symptom.

Then I became a mama myself, and it changed how I practice. I know what it feels like to sit on the other side of the exam room, worried and wanting someone to just tell you, clearly and kindly, what's going on and what to do next. That's the doctor I try to be for you.

"You'll never get a rushed answer or a lecture from me. Just honest guidance and a plan we make together."

Dr. Mariam

Health notes

Little tips from Dr. Mariam

Quick, practical advice for everyday family health.

Kids

Fever isn't the enemy

A fever is the body fighting back. Watch how your child is acting more than the number. Call if they're under 3 months, very drowsy, or struggling to drink.

Wellness

Protect your sleep

Screens off 30 minutes before bed and a steady wake-up time do more for mood, focus and blood sugar than most supplements.

Everyday

Sip before you snack

Thirst often feels like hunger or a headache. Keep a water bottle in sight, especially for kids after school and on busy workdays.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major insurance plans (list to be confirmed). Self-pay options are also available with transparent pricing. Call or email us with your plan name and we'll verify coverage before your visit.

What types of visits do you offer?

In-office visits for exams, check-ups and sick visits; video visits for follow-ups and many common concerns; and non-urgent questions through the "Ask Dr. Mariam" form.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. This website, the question form and email are not monitored for emergencies and should never be used for urgent medical needs.

Do you see children and newborns?

Yes! Family medicine means all ages. Dr. Mariam sees newborns, kids, teens, adults and older adults, so the whole family can share one doctor.

Can I get a same-day appointment?

We hold time each day for sick visits. Call in the morning for the best chance of a same-day slot, or request one through the booking form below.
